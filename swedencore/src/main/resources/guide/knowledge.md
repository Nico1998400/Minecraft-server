NORDIA is a persistent player-driven society on Minecraft. The fantasy is "I am building a life in this world".
There is no single correct path: employee, freelancer, company owner, hermit or investor are all valid. The economy
rewards scale, specialisation and cooperation; players need each other. Minecraft is the world, NORDIA's systems are
the game.

THREE SEPARATE THINGS
- Skill = what you can do. It grows from real work (mining, farming, herbalism, building, forestry, fishing,
  engineering, logistics). Idle (AFK) time and repetitive loops give little or nothing.
- Reputation = how much other players trust you. It grows from finishing contracts and paying wages, and falls when you
  abandon contracts, miss wages or get evicted.
- Money = economic success. The currency is kronor (SEK).

MONEY
- Everyone gets one-time starter money. After that money only moves between players; nothing is printed out of thin air.
- There is NO auction house and no /ah. Trade happens in physical shops, through contracts, buy orders and direct trades.

COMMANDS AND MENUS (the player opens /tutorial: a menu that explains every part of the game, with a button to the full action menu)
- /tutorial: the tutorial menu (topics: how Nordia works, money, jobs, companies, contracts, trade, land and towns, skills and reputation, homes and travel, help). /meny (or /menu): the action menu with a button for every system, also reachable from the tutorial. /spawn: teleport to spawn. /language: switch Swedish/English.
- /balance: money. /pay: pay another player. /transactions: money history.
- /profile: identity, reputation, first job. /skills: skill levels and XP.
- /jobs: the job board; apply to companies, go on duty to be paid for working. Wages are paid for verified (non-AFK) work.
- /company: found and run companies (founding costs a registration fee), hire staff, finances, bankruptcy.
- /contracts and /contract: escrowed jobs. The issuer's reward is locked in escrow when the contract is created, so
  contractors can trust it. Item deliveries go to the issuer's stash; service contracts are confirmed by the issuer.
- /stash: your item storage (also companies have one). Warehouses add capacity.
- /shop and /shops find <item>: physical shops (right-click a chest to buy). /shops find lists who sells an item.
- /orders and /order: buy orders; a buyer escrows a budget and many sellers can fill it.
- /trade: face-to-face trade window with another nearby player.
- /market <item>: price statistics. /production: recipes run in factories, mines, farms and industrial land.
- /transports and /transport: logistics jobs, carry cargo for pay.
- /property: own or rent land and buildings (shops, factories, warehouses, homes). /settlement: found an outpost that can
  grow into a village, town and city when it has enough members and money.
- /loan: lend and borrow. /shares: companies have shares, dividends and investors.
- /texturer: optional texture pack.

GETTING STARTED (a good first path for a new player)
1. Look around the spawn town and open /tutorial.
2. Find a job with /jobs or a contract with /contracts, and do real work to earn money and skill.
3. Sell what you make in a shop or through contracts, and meet other players.
4. Grow: specialise in a skill, join or found a company, rent or buy property, join or found a settlement.
